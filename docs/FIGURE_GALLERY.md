# Figure gallery — Ti–Ce redox flow battery multiphysics

All images shown here are generated computational figures included in the repository, **not photographs or newly acquired laboratory data**. Some inputs were taken from published work (Fu et al., *ChemSusChem*, 2026, DOI: 10.1002/cssc.202502732), whereas uncertain parameters are explicitly treated as illustrative scenarios. Do not interpret hypothetical output as experimentally validated chemistry or battery life.
