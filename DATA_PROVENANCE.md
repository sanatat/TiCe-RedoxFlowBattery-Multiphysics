# Scientific provenance and boundaries

**Primary paper:** Fu et al. (2026), *ChemSusChem*, DOI [10.1002/cssc.202502732](https://doi.org/10.1002/cssc.202502732), plus the companion 41-page Supporting Information (SI). Original PDFs are intentionally not redistributed.

## Model-derived (not experimental) outputs

- Predicted 2D concentrations, wall fluxes and transport coefficients for an *assumed* 30 mm × 1 mm × 30 mm channel, including numerical mass conservation checks.
- LBM velocity fields for periodic idealized channels and baffles in lattice units.
- Effective Thiele and porous-felt parameters under chosen internal area and tortuosity scenarios.
- Synthetic voltammograms, EIS equivalent circuits, and mass-balanced reservoir cycling; not collected from devices.
- Phenomenological Figure 6c voltage fit, 10 estimated training points and 4 *same-curve* holdout points; no raw experiment validation.
- Figures and CSV tables are generated by the original scripts; numerical predictions and uncertainties must not be attributed to Fu et al.

## Identifiability and non-validation

The Figure 6c reduced-order parameter `j_curvature_mA_cm2` is a *fit curvature scale*, not a physically validated limiting current. Excluding the last visually read point causes the inferred parameter to reach the optimizer bound; the data cannot uniquely separate current limits, ohmic loss, kinetics and true felt active area. Conditional pressure/power extrapolations require measured Δp(Q), real geometry and electrolyte velocity fields for physical validation.

## How to use this study ethically

Suggested description: “Independent Python simulation of Ti–Ce redox flow battery transport and electrochemical performance using published experimental parameters, including finite-volume mass conservation, model sensitivity and inverse-parameter identifiability.”

Do not claim new physical battery experiments, measured CV/EIS, a reproduction of the 3,721-cycle experiment, author partnership, new DFT calculations or a validated experimental digital twin.

## Added gap-coverage extensions (unmeasured scenarios)

- `extensions/chemistry.py` uses the 0.5 M Ti + 0.5 M Ce + 2 M MSA SI baseline. Sulfate fraction, mixed-acid conductivity/viscosity multipliers, 1:1 conditional ligand-binding coefficients and intrinsic saturation-capacity thresholds are **hypothetical**. No chemical equilibrium constant, solid-phase solubility product, activity coefficient, or experimental mixed-acid data was supplied; output is **not** a Ti/Ce precipitation prediction.
- `extensions/reactor.py` uses published cell area, flow and apparent CV-based diffusivity/kinetic numbers; the felt permeability, 1D flow path, effective reactive area, film thickness, current-utilization factor and contact resistance are **assumed**.  The axial reactor is coupled at local species/voltage balance level but is **not** a spatially resolved Butler–Volmer porous-electrode CFD validation.
- `extensions/membrane.py` uses reported Ce H-cell permeability as an anchor but transplants it to **assumed full-cell geometry**. Ti permeability, effective charges of complexes, imposed membrane potential and water flow are **assumed**. It preserves **Ti and Ce inventories**, but does not solve proton migration/electroneutrality.
- `extensions/stack.py` assumes Darcy linear parallel hydraulic branches and series-connected electric cells; no manufacturer stack measurements, shunt currents or manifold CAD.
- `extensions/lifetime.py` uses **illustrative** reversible imbalance, felt ageing and irreversible inventory-fade rates. It is **not fitted** to the 3,721-cycle measurements; 'remixing' represents an idealized intervention with unknown energy/time cost.

The new figures carry labels such as *scenario*, *illustrative*, *not measured*, or *not prediction* to prevent conflating computational examples with published experiments.
